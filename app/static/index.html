  1. Ensure config/credentials.json is a Desktop OAuth client with redirect http://localhost.
  2. Click Connect Google Drive and sign in with the account that can open the shares.
  3. After Allow, the browser shows "localhost refused to connect" — that is expected.
  4. Copy the entire address bar, paste it below, and click Finish.

You only need to do this once until the token expires or is revoked.
